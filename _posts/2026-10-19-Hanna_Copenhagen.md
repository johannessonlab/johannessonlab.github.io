Hanna was in Copenhagen to serve as one of the opponents (together with Duur Aanen) of Asta Rødsgaard-Jørgensen’s thesis on the interesting fungus Leucoagaricus gongylophorus, which is farmed by the leaf-cutter ants. She also gave a talk on the germline sequestration in _Marasmius_ fungi.
