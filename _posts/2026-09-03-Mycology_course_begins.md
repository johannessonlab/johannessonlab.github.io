The evening course on Fungal Biology (Svamparnas biologi) given at SU started today. We had 32 fungal enthusiasts in the group, wanting to learn all there is to know about fungi. We have also launched the open homepage that will be used as a resource in this course and that can also be used by other interested. See the course webpage here: [https://svamparnasbiologi.org/](https://svamparnasbiologi.org/)
